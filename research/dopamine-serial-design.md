# Dopamine-first serial design — research synthesis

**Purpose:** define a deliberately pleasure-heavy authoring overlay for a second Korean martial/progression web novel. This does not replace the general [`fiction-craft-standard.md`](../docs/fiction-craft-standard.md); it changes the project’s intended experience and reward cadence.

## Evidence boundary

“Dopamine rush” is useful reader language, not a claim that a plot device directly causes a measurable neurotransmitter release.

Reward-prediction-error research describes learning signals and also emphasizes that dopamine has diverse functions beyond one simple pleasure mechanism.[5]

Research on curiosity and competence provides constraints and analogies, not a formula that guarantees enjoyment.[6][9]

Research on hedonic variety and humor is similarly indirect for fiction design.[7][8]

The practical target is therefore:

> **clear expectation → active anticipation → earned payoff → visible state change → a fresh, specific question**

Each installment should complete at least one reward loop while advancing larger ones.

## What successful Korean examples make legible

The official premises repeatedly combine **low apparent status** with an **exclusive asymmetry**:

- *Return of the Blossoming Blade* places a former Mount Hua master in a child’s body and turns private mastery into the collective restoration of a fallen sect.[2][12][14]
- *Return of the Mad Demon* combines regression knowledge with an unpredictable temperament, so readers expect hierarchy reversal without knowing its exact form.[15][16]
- *Nano Machine* gives a low-ranked heir a future nanomachine inside a succession contest, making diagnosis, training correction, knowledge, and advancement discrete rewards.[4][17]
- *Absolute Sword Sense* gives a discarded spy both regression knowledge and the ability to hear a dagger, so acquisitions create information and tactical options rather than only larger numbers.[3][18]
- *Best Teacher Baek* converts hidden mastery into repeated student transformations, professional recognition, and institutional growth.[19]
- *Infinite Leveling: Murim* makes effort-to-reward conversion explicit through quests and levels.[20]

The legal first chapter of *Heavenly Demon Can’t Live a Normal Life* demonstrates the compact hidden-competence loop particularly clearly: observers compare the old “fool” with a newly authoritative man, he deliberately gathers local information, intervenes in an abuse, asks what law applies, and ends by declaring that he will execute the sentence.[1]

These examples do not prove that regression or systems are intrinsically superior. They show how quickly a serial can establish:

1. what everyone thinks the protagonist is;
2. what advantage the protagonist actually possesses;
3. what immediate hierarchy can be overturned;
4. what larger ladder becomes available afterward.

## Durable reward engines

### 1. Loaded underestimation

A complete hidden-competence payoff has three stages:

1. **Mispricing:** an authority confidently assigns the protagonist low value.
2. **Specific proof:** the protagonist notices or performs something rank cannot explain.
3. **Social update:** witnesses change access, allegiance, caution, surveillance, or status.

An endless queue of foolish young masters is not progression. Every reveal must permanently alter the social map.

### 2. Knowledge as direction, not answer key

Regression and reincarnation replace “Can the hero understand this?” with “How will the hero exploit or change it?” They also create anticipation before action. They become stale when memory supplies every artifact, betrayal, and solution perfectly.

For this project, every use of prior-loop knowledge must encounter at least one of:

- a future already changed by the protagonist;
- an incomplete remembered fact;
- a new obligation or enemy produced by exploitation;
- a choice between gain and saving someone;
- a genuinely unknown event after the remembered period.

### 3. Visible competence loops

Self-determination theory identifies autonomy, competence, and relatedness as central needs supporting high-quality motivation and engagement.[9] The serial equivalent is:

> learn or remember → choose an approach → attempt → receive feedback → adapt → demonstrate mastery

Every advancement needs:

- an input: risk, practice, resource, observation, or sacrifice;
- a visible delta: a newly possible action;
- a test within the current or next chapter;
- a new ceiling after the test.

### 4. Anticipation plus recognition

Narrative research distinguishes suspense, curiosity, and surprise through how event information is ordered; both known and unknown outcomes can produce appreciation through different routes.[11] Curiosity research also finds that moderate confidence often creates stronger curiosity than complete ignorance.[6]

Therefore:

- tell readers enough to predict the protagonist’s intended win;
- conceal or complicate one decisive element;
- deliver the promised win rather than replacing it with another delay;
- add a causally earned secondary benefit or implication;
- close small information gaps in the same scene or chapter.

A cliffhanger should change the reader’s model of the story, not stop halfway through an ordinary sword swing.

### 5. Progress made public

Progress-monitoring interventions improve goal attainment across many domains, particularly when progress is recorded or reported.[27] Goal-gradient research also supports the practical value of making proximity to a reward visible.[28]

Useful world-state markers include:

- opened seals or nodes;
- acquired techniques with demonstrated uses;
- new titles, uniforms, or access rights;
- debt or ownership changes;
- allies who now act differently;
- a named rank or public reputation;
- territory, institution, or faction capacity.

A number without qualitative consequence is not a satisfying progression marker.

### 6. Variable reward currency

Positive gains can lose impact through adaptation; research on hedonic variety reports associations between varied pleasurable spending and well-being while remaining cautious about causal direction.[7] The fiction-design implication is modest: vary the *kind* of satisfaction instead of making every chapter a larger version of the same fight.

Rotate among:

1. **Power:** technique, bodily repair, breakthrough.
2. **Resource:** treasure, money, medicine, information.
3. **Status:** witnessed revaluation, title, access.
4. **Relationship:** rescue, loyalty, rival respect, faction growth.
5. **Control:** a mystery answered, trap predicted, future changed.
6. **Comedy:** arrogance punctured through concise competence.
7. **Spectacle:** an earned impossible-looking feat.

Do not use the same dominant reward currency three chapters in a row.

### 7. Comedy as compact status victory

Benign-violation research stresses that a violation must also be perceived as benign, with power and cultural context affecting that judgment.[8] In this project, humor should punch upward at arrogance, pretension, or an abuser’s certainty—not downward at helplessness.

A comic status reversal should:

1. establish who controls the interaction;
2. let that person expose a blind spot;
3. give the underestimated character a short, competent reversal;
4. show witnesses updating their judgment;
5. stop once the hierarchy has changed.

## Project-specific cadence

The following numbers are design constraints for this experiment, not scientific universals:

- meaningful micro-payoff roughly every **500–900 Korean characters**;
- medium reversal, acquisition, or recognition every **1,500–2,000 characters**;
- at least one undeniable major victory per chapter;
- most setup paid within the same chapter;
- no promised reward left untouched for more than one chapter;
- no more than two consecutive explanatory paragraphs without pressure, humor, discovery, or gain;
- every loss begins a productive comeback immediately.

### Six fields for every chapter

1. **Near promise:** what satisfaction is signaled in the opening quarter?
2. **Reader prediction:** what does the reader reasonably expect?
3. **Agency test:** what choice or competence earns movement?
4. **Delivered reward:** what is conclusively gained, answered, or reversed?
5. **Visible update:** what remains permanently different?
6. **Next gap:** what new, specific reward or threat follows?

## Selected concept

### 《회귀자 일곱이 죽고 점소이만 남았다》

Seven martial heroes repeated the same thirty days 9,431 times. The inn boy they treated as a disposable background figure was the return formation’s recording vessel. When all seven die and the loop finally ends, he remembers every failed technique, hidden cache, betrayal, and route—but his knowledge stops at day thirty. He must recover seven foundations to let his present body express what nine thousand repetitions taught him.

This keeps familiar regression pleasures while changing the owner of the fantasy:

- protagonist is not a regressor;
- the loop is over before Chapter 1;
- prior knowledge covers only a bounded period;
- deviations quickly produce genuine uncertainty;
- mastery comes from accumulated observation and pain, while physical capacity still requires visible unlocks;
- the final victory requires an original action no loop recorded.

The repeating engine is:

> recognize rehearsed situation → predict or preempt → gain resource/status → unlock stored proficiency → deviation creates unknown problem → synthesize an original answer

## Anti-pattern release gate

Reject or revise a chapter if:

- it withholds the advertised win only to prolong serialization;
- a power-up lacks input, test, or visible consequence;
- the protagonist wins because everyone else becomes stupid;
- witnesses gasp but social reality does not change;
- future knowledge remains perfectly reliable after divergence;
- reward inventory grows faster than dramatized use;
- three consecutive payoffs use the same currency;
- humiliation targets weakness rather than arrogance;
- the next hook is merely “a stronger enemy appeared”;
- a quiet aftermath fails to show how the last reward changed daily life.
