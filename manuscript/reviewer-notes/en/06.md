# Chapter 6 Editorial Overview

During the three-day descent, Jin Cheol extends Unraveling Qi to ten breaths and reaches the beginning of the third-rate tier, but his injuries remain and he cannot contest late third-rate Do Changsu head-on. At Mulle Valley, twelve grain sacks and twelve-year-old Kangho wait beneath the escort agency’s flag as payment. Yeonhwa stops Jin Cheol from starting an immediate fight and wins two days to prepare, while Do states the concrete pressure on his side: failure to meet his quota will endanger his sister and nephew inside the agency.

Yeonhwa compares seventy-eight seom paid by the village over two years with receipts for only forty-one, then distributes copies through three independent routes: nine victim villages, Bak Munsu’s official filing date, and Cheongha Escort Agency’s contract office. Destroying the local ledger can no longer erase the testimony, government trail, or threat of merchant-contract loss. Do’s subordinates Bak Seok and Yu Gap find debts falsified under their relatives’ names and refuse orders, while seventeen households approve the resonance implement’s use and contribute coordinated hammer and shuttle noise.

The confrontation rigorously observes the power limits. Mind’s Eye detects Do’s broad Qi flow, Unraveling Qi remains inside Jin Cheol to support balance and endurance, and Drifting Step supplies only a half-step developed from repeated observation. The nails, chisel, well stone, and villagers’ blows transmit physical vibration into Do’s wrist; his reflexive correction collides with his own circulation rhythm and makes him drop the saber. Jin Cheol neither projects Unraveling Qi nor damages Do’s meridians or dantian, and he pays with a broken rib before demanding the tribute ledger rather than Do’s life.

Do could still win the fight, but withdraws after calculating document exposure, contract loss, subordinate refusal, and retaliation against his family. Mulle Valley bans exchanging children for debt, protection fees, or entrance fees, lets the rescued children choose whether and where to return, and divides evidence and keys so no individual owns the victory. The escort agency remains intact, Jin Cheol admits he does not know whether he would permanently depower Do if able, and Cheon Muryeong closes the volume by correlating dates, people, metal sounds, and the three nails across reports—carefully labeling Qi involvement unproven before beginning an investigation.
