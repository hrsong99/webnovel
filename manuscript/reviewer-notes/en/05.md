# Chapter 5 Editorial Overview

Jin Cheol, Yeonhwa, and Seo Muhan follow unnatural wind and the seal’s vibration to the Archive of Forsaken Obsessions on Blackwind Mountain. Jin Cheol opens its five-metal lock not by ranking strength abstractly, but by placing iron outside to receive impact and soft tin inside to deform and absorb what remains. Making craftsmanship and stress distribution the admission test renews the novel’s promise that material knowledge, not latent martial genius, is Jin Cheol’s first advantage.

The resonance chamber reflects each visitor’s strongest attachment. Jin Cheol sees the smithy before the disaster and is tempted both by the fantasy of saving his parents and by the chance to reexamine the vibrations he missed. Yeonhwa forces him to choose the living present; he breaks the mechanism while hearing his parents die again, and she and Jin Cheol must then pull Seo away from his dead son. Passing seals the entrance behind them, giving the emotional choice an irreversible physical cost.

The Sutra of Unraveling is not an instant depowering manual. It requires the joint development of Mind’s Eye, Unraveling Qi, and the Form-Shifting Art whose first method is Drifting Step. At the current Perception stage, Unraveling Qi can only support sensation and posture inside Jin Cheol’s body; external interference requires first-rate cultivation, and permanent dissolution of a dantian requires the transcendent realm after bodily transformation. Jin Cheol deliberately reopens the scar that almost formed at Cheonggye and sustains one thread of Unraveling Qi for only three breaths before vomiting, explicitly blocking sudden mastery.

Yeonhwa recognizes that Jin Cheol’s request to be stopped still leaves him claiming final judgment. She therefore secures concrete limits: she keeps the original scripture, Seo keeps the resonance implement, and use on a person requires prior approval from Yeonhwa, Seo, and a majority of the affected victims. The three nails recovered with Jin Cheol’s parents become a training instrument, not a weapon. A message that Do will take doubled protection fees, grain, and one child from Mulle Valley in three days forces this incomplete discipline into immediate use.
