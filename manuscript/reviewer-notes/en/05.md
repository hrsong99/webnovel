# Chapter 5 Editorial Overview

The five-metal lock at the Archive of Forsaken Obsessions opens through a blacksmith’s understanding of impact distribution rather than hidden martial talent. Inside are the names of practitioners killed not while seeking greater power but while trying to give it up. The entrance therefore pays off the novel’s promise that Jin Cheol’s practical knowledge of material and stress is a genuine source of agency.

The resonance chamber offers Yeonhwa an intact hand and her old loom before it offers Jin Cheol the smithy before the disaster and his living parents. Yeonhwa notices that the illusion’s weaving is too perfect, breaks her own vision, and then tells Jin Cheol to listen to the living. He turns from his parents and destroys the resonant latch while hearing them die again.

The Unbinding Scripture is not an instant severance technique. Jin Cheol can presently learn only Mind’s Eye, the internally confined Unraveling Qi, and Drifting Step. Interference beyond the body lies past first-class cultivation, while safe dantian dissolution lies beyond Marrow Rebirth. He remains slower than Do Changsu and cannot remove anyone’s power.

To cultivate, Jin Cheol consciously reopens the very wound he condemns and retains one hair-thin thread of Unraveling Qi for three breaths before vomiting. Yeonhwa imposes enforceable custody: original leaves, copy, and resonance tool stay in separate hands, and human use requires advance consent from her, Seo, and a majority of affected people. When word arrives that Do will take grain and a child from Mulle Valley in three days, they descend carrying different burdens in the same direction.
