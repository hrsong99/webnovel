# Chapter 3 Editorial Overview

Two years later, Jin Cheol has been rejected by seventeen sects because of his age, dull sensitivity to Qi, and damaged body. He enters Cheongu Hall, a branch institution of Unmun Escort Agency, as an itinerant smith to learn how martial artists are produced. From Mulle Valley, where she runs the survivors’ ledger and information network, Yeonhwa sends reports that children purchased entrance with family money and never returned. Their betrothal remains intact but indefinitely postponed until they renegotiate how they would live together and who has the right to stop whom.

In the cellar, seven children are repeatedly submerged, hauled up by the hair for one mouthful of air, then thrust under again to tear open their dantians through terror and suffocation. Jin Cheol ignores Yeonhwa’s warning not to confuse verification with rescue and intervenes immediately, only to be overwhelmed by Do Changsu, a late third-rate fighter. Do argues that he survived an even worse initiation to feed his mother and that a crueler replacement would take his place, making him both an active abuser and a lower link in the same extraction system.

The fight’s real payoff is observational: Jin Cheol hears Do’s breath, planted heel, and vibrating scabbard align in a “two, one, pause” cycle. Knowing the pattern does not let his injured, ordinary body answer it, so perception does not substitute for cultivation or speed. Seo Muhan succeeds where Jin Cheol cannot because he has already widened a drain, arranged Mrs. Obok’s cart, and prepared wet-mugwort smoke; six children escape, while the child already limp in the vat remains an irreversible loss.

Seo identifies Jin Cheol’s burn scar as the trace of a dantian that nearly opened during the Cheonggye disaster and then closed because he never learned to keep it open. What opened instead was Jin Cheol’s sensitivity to discordant weapon sounds during Qi circulation. Cheongu Hall now knows his face, and Do’s blade has marked the cart’s rear axle, so the rescue closes his undercover route and immediately creates a pursuit problem.
