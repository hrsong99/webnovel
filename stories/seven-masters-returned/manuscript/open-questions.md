# Open questions before source drafting

Only unresolved decisions belong here. Accepted premise rules live in `story-bible.md` and must not be reopened accidentally.

## Blocking for Volume One outline

1. Lock the final Korean and English series titles, or explicitly authorize the current working titles.
2. Lock Ryu’s exact opening role: 점소이, junior manager, adopted family worker, or another concrete legal/social status under Ma Yeongsun.
3. Lock Ma Yeongsun’s opening age and what she knows about Ryu’s future relationship with her.
4. Define the exact failed-future scene in which the seven select Ryu and the cost that leaves their echoes intact.
5. Define the first return date and which original-future events occur during Volume One.
6. Lock Baek Mugyeol’s younger age, rank, faction name, and formative leadership mistake.
7. Lock Tang Soryeong’s younger age, family structure, faction name, and formative medical crisis.
8. Decide whether the first two awakenings occur in Chapters 1–2 or across a longer first volume.
9. Decide the exact legal trigger for an inner guest-room door to open; it must be dramatic and testable rather than arbitrary leveling.
10. Define what portion of an awakened core Ryu can use immediately and the physical cost of over-channeling.
11. Define the first durable cooperation artifact produced by Baek and Tang’s factions.
12. Decide whether Myogak remains as a neutral/hostile formation custodian, is redesigned, or is omitted.

## Blocking before long-range outline

1. Name and characterize the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult’s doctrine, governance, territorial base, and reasons some people accept it.
2. Decide whether a single Heavenly Demon exists as a major character while remaining only one part of the institutional conflict.
3. Lock the younger starting states and formative failures of Gwi Sam, Maehwajin, Jang Cheolwoo, Yeom Gaha, and Seol Muyeon.
4. Define how time skips are distributed across fifteen years.
5. Define how the seven echoes change emotionally after their personal arcs instead of becoming static advisers.
6. Decide whether Ma Yeongsun ever hears or enters the inner Return Inn.
7. Define whether brief echo/younger synchronization is legal, its consent ritual, and its cost.
8. Define what Seol Muyeon withheld and why the others still trusted the final return.
9. Define the eighth path’s observable foundation without fixing its final technique too early.

## Non-blocking experiments

- Whether chapter epigraphs may use contradictory comments from awakened masters.
- Whether the inner inn physically improves as coalition capabilities are created.
- Whether Ma charges the spirits imaginary room fees after learning about them.
- Whether the retired `return-ink-v1` palette can be adapted after new character references are approved.
