# Chapter 1 overview

In the failed future, the seven strongest survivors spend the only return on Ryu Dan because he is the sole neutral person they all trust. Ryu wakes fifteen years earlier, finds Ma Yeongsun alive, and meets future Baek Mugyeol in the first open room of his inner Return Inn.

When the Black Tooth Gang arrives with a forged lien, Ryu prevents the accident that originally destroyed Ma’s knee. He rejects future Baek’s order to kill, uses the one available seven-heartbeat channel to defeat Oh Gak’s collection party without fatalities, and takes the original debt ledger instead of pursuing the gang.

Ma retains ownership and custody of the evidence. Ryu carries a persistent hand tremor from the borrowed channel. The expelled Oh Gak returns from the eastern warehouse poisoned and holding an Azure Peak token, opening an immediate choice between preserving evidence and saving an enemy.
