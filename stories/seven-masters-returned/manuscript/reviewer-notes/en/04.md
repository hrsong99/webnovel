# Chapter 4 overview

Living Tang separates fact from inference and discovers that the current Green Vein Poison contains a refined resin binder absent from her future echo’s expected formula. Future knowledge provides principles, but only present testing reveals the changed threat.

Future Tang urges Ryu to steal her family’s completed prescription, while living Tang prepares to test poison on herself. Ryu refuses both forms of unilateral sacrifice. He preserves living Tang’s authority by proposing observation of Oh Gak and separate tests of the current sample.

Living Tang chooses bounded disclosure: the full antidote remains Tang property, while a safe emergency protocol becomes shareable. Ma identifies the flaw in keeping only separate Tang and Azure Peak copies, prompting a jointly sealed Return Inn copy. The discovery that the poison is designed for heated market oil forces a joint warehouse operation.
