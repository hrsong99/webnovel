# Chapter 6 overview

With both borrowed channels spent and his meridians torn, Ryu remains at Return Inn’s counter. Nam’s route map, living Tang’s emergency protocol, Oh Gak’s gang signals, and Ma’s expense ledger become one distributed response. Living Baek honors Nam’s delegated command, and living Tang divides her protocol without surrendering medical authority.

Oh Gak uses the deputy’s habits and gang bells to expose the last poison cart. When it rolls toward the inn, Ryu uses no borrowed core. He redirects it with his own body and Returning Dish Step, while the living Ma throws the wet mat that extinguishes the fuse.

No one dies. Azure Peak accepts escort duties, the Tang Family accepts medicine and replenishment duties, and Ma controls keys, records, emergency access, and costs through the Common Antidote Ledger. Future Baek and Tang remain active at the same inner table. A single involuntary fragment leaks through Gwi Sam’s still-closed door, creating the next arc without awakening him.
