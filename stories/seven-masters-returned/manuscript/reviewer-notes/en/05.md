# Chapter 5 overview

Nam commands routes and exits, living Tang controls poison and patients, and living Baek handles internal traps. Oh Gak’s testimony, Ryu’s reconstruction of future ruins, and current reconnaissance point to three different entrances because saving the witness has already changed the warehouse.

Black Tooth’s deputy uses workers and poison oil as hostages. A mixed-energy formation turns competing martial traditions into a trap. Living Baek abandons pursuit to extinguish the torch, while living Tang evacuates workers. Ryu channels both cores for two safe shared heartbeats and a tearing third overdraw, redirecting rather than merging their forces.

Keeping a poison barrel level creates Ryu’s first original art, Returning Dish Step. A relay line triggers Myogak beneath Return Inn without moving the custodian to the warehouse. Western standardization marks and One Heaven, One Measure reveal the larger institutional threat. The chapter ends with Ryu injured and poisoned market oil already in circulation.
