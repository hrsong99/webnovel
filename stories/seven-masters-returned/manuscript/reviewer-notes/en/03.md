# Chapter 3 overview

Ryu gives Ma a bounded truth about his future knowledge and accepts her three-day deadline and control over further strange power inside the inn. Ma then forces Azure Peak to issue a written receipt before inspecting the token.

The twenty-two-year-old living Baek challenges Ryu to a three-exchange, no-internal-energy test. Future Baek predicts the first form, but his younger self adapts immediately. Ryu wins the final exchange without reopening the core, deriving an original sidestep from a tilting tea tray. The duel proves that a future echo is not automatically superior to the living person.

Ryu returns the token under receipt and secures bounded cooperation, continued evidence custody, and a joint investigation. Living Baek delegates route command to Nam. Tang Soryeong’s younger self arrives at sunset and orders Azure Peak away from the poison seal.
