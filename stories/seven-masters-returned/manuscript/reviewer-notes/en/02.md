# Chapter 2 overview

Ryu accepts Ma’s quarantine, water, tool, and wage conditions before treating Oh Gak in the isolated jar room. Future Baek argues for preserving the body and Azure Peak token as evidence, but Ryu points out that Azure Peak took the corpse in the failed future and still failed to identify the poison.

When Ryu chooses the living patient over an uncontaminated sample, Tang Soryeong’s future echo awakens. The two masters immediately disagree, yet Ryu imposes speaking order and combines Tang’s diagnosis with Baek’s breath count. Oh Gak survives and reveals the warehouse bell code, hidden ledger, and western exit.

Ma locks the token and poison sample in separate boxes. Oh Gak’s survival invalidates the exact future schedule. The chapter ends when Azure Peak investigator Nam Sogun arrives to arrest Ryu for token theft and stolen martial knowledge.
