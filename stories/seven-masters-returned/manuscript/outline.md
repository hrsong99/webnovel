# Outline state — series locked, Volume One not yet authorized

**Status:** macro architecture accepted; chapter-level causal outline must be reviewed before prose drafting.

## Series spine

- Failed future: fifteen years from the opening, the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult has conquered the fragmented martial world.
- Return choice: seven dying masters select neutral innkeeper Ryu Dan for the one available return.
- Opening state: Ryu wakes in his twenty-year-old body; Baek Mugyeol’s echo is the first active voice; six inner guest rooms remain locked.
- Early engine: awaken masters sequentially, meet younger versions, prevent defining failures, and create one coalition capability per arc.
- Middle engine: institutional cooperation creates historical divergence and invalidates exact future details.
- Late engine: the changed coalition faces an earlier, adaptive invasion.
- Ending proof: living masters and organizations cooperate voluntarily; Ryu’s eighth path arises from coordination rather than archived technique.

## Provisional first-volume jobs

These are design jobs, not approved chapter beats.

1. Establish the failed future, unanimous choice of Ryu, one-time return, Baek’s awakening, Ma’s survival, and an immediate locally overpowered reversal.
2. Prove that one future archive is partial rather than omniscient; introduce a poison crisis that begins Tang Soryeong’s awakening.
3. Bring future Baek into conflict with younger Baek; Ryu must win access without reducing the younger master to an obedient asset.
4. Bring future Tang and younger Tang into focus through a medical choice involving real consent and shared knowledge.
5. Force the two younger masters or their factions into a first bounded joint operation; the cooperation must change custody or command.
6. Deliver a concrete victory against an early precursor of the coming conquest, establish Return Inn’s first neutral function, and end with the third door responding.

## Required chapter-outline fields

Before drafting, each chapter must specify:

- opening promise;
- POV and immediate want;
- pressure and opposing agency;
- future memory legally available;
- current divergence from the archive;
- choice that closes another option;
- local payoff;
- persistent object, injury, debt, access, status, or relationship delta;
- coalition capability advanced;
- next pressure.
