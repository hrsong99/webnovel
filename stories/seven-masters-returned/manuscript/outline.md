# Volume One causal outline — 두 개의 열린 객방

**Status:** locked for six Korean source chapters. Each chapter must satisfy the contract below before it is considered drafted.

## Volume promise

Ryu returns once, awakens future Baek and Tang, changes their younger selves’ first failed cooperation, and turns Return Inn into the neutral custodian of a real shared antidote system. The volume ends after a complete local victory with Gwi Sam’s room beginning to wake.

## Chapter 1 — 마지막 손님이 돌아왔다

- **Near promise:** The seven strongest survivors choose an inn worker for the only return; within the chapter Ryu uses Baek’s first seven heartbeats to stop Ma’s injury and defeat Black Tooth’s collectors.
- **POV / want:** Ryu wants proof that Ma is alive and to keep Black Tooth from seizing the inn.
- **Pressure:** His twenty-year-old body has no internal energy; future Baek is awake but controlling; the forged lien appears legally valid.
- **Legal future knowledge:** Ryu personally remembers Ma’s knee being crushed and the storage-yard seizure. Future Baek recognizes the collector’s borrowed Azure Peak sword structure.
- **Divergence:** Ryu intervenes before the falling bowl and refuses Baek’s order to kill the lead collector.
- **Choice:** He spends the one daily seven-heartbeat channel to defeat rather than execute, then takes the lien ledger as evidence instead of pursuing the fleeing gang.
- **Local payoff:** Ma is unhurt; the collectors are disarmed with tray, bowl, and minimal sword structure; their authority collapses publicly.
- **Delta:** Baek’s first room is open; Ma holds the Black Tooth lien ledger; Black Tooth loses immediate possession but will return.
- **Coalition progress:** None yet; Ryu establishes that he will not obey a future master automatically.
- **Persistence:** Meridian tremor in Ryu’s right hand; one channel spent; Ma’s suspicion; defeated collector Oh Gak is expelled and returns to the warehouse.
- **Next pressure:** A poisoned courier collapses outside the inn carrying Azure Peak’s token.

## Chapter 2 — 독은 혼자만의 비밀이 아니다

- **Near promise:** Ryu discovers that Baek’s archive cannot explain the poison and awakens Tang Soryeong by choosing to save an enemy courier.
- **POV / want:** Ryu wants the courier alive long enough to identify the poison route and protect the inn.
- **Pressure:** Baek recommends preserving evidence rather than risking contamination; Ma refuses uncontrolled treatment in her dining room; the courier is Black Tooth-affiliated and hostile.
- **Legal future knowledge:** Ryu remembers the courier died in the failed week. Baek remembers confiscating the body but never learned the poison formula. Dormant Tang memory responds only after Ryu makes a different choice.
- **Divergence:** The courier survives and identifies a warehouse bell code; the future schedule immediately becomes unreliable.
- **Choice:** Ryu accepts Ma’s explicit terms—isolated jar room, contaminated tools boiled, all cost charged to him, no patient in the dining room—and treats Oh Gak rather than taking the token and letting him die.
- **Local payoff:** Tang’s door opens; her first line contradicts Baek; their argument produces a workable emergency treatment.
- **Delta:** Tang echo awake; Azure Peak token in Ma’s locked box; sealed Green Vein residue; courier Oh Gak becomes a witness.
- **Coalition progress:** First shared diagnosis between incompatible future experts, mediated by Ryu and governed materially by Ma.
- **Persistence:** Ryu cannot channel Baek again that day; Tang core is unstable; exact future sequence is broken.
- **Next pressure:** Azure Peak examiner Nam Sogun arrives to arrest Ryu for Baek’s token and stolen sword knowledge.

## Chapter 3 — 나보다 어린 검성

- **Near promise:** Future Baek must watch Ryu confront his proud twenty-two-year-old self; Ryu publicly defeats an Azure Peak form without claiming to be stronger than the sect.
- **POV / want:** Ryu wants three days of Azure Peak cooperation and legal recognition of Ma’s evidence custody.
- **Pressure:** Younger Baek demands the token, the living witness, and the poison evidence; Nam can close the road; revealing the future would make Ryu a target; future Baek wants his younger self forced into obedience.
- **Legal future knowledge:** Future Baek knows his own foundational sequence and the habit of taking command from Nam. Ryu knows the failed warehouse raid’s route only from his own later reconstruction.
- **Divergence:** Younger Baek adapts after seeing the first correction, so the duel cannot follow archived moves exactly.
- **Choice:** Ryu stops using future Baek’s called counters and redirects a tea tray’s tilt into an original sidestep; he returns the token under written receipt instead of bargaining with theft.
- **Local payoff:** He wins a bounded three-exchange test and exposes that the poison evidence belongs to both medicine and security.
- **Delta:** Azure Peak recognizes Return Inn’s temporary custody; Nam receives route command; younger Baek agrees to a three-day joint investigation but not Ryu’s authority.
- **Coalition progress:** First written limit on Baek’s command and first delegation to Nam.
- **Persistence:** Younger Baek is intrigued and angry; future Baek is embarrassed; Ryu’s tray-redirection idea begins.
- **Next pressure:** Tang Soryeong arrives and refuses to let Azure Peak open the poison seal.

## Chapter 4 — 미래의 독왕은 아직 의원이었다

- **Near promise:** Future Tang meets the compassionate younger physician she claims never existed; Ryu must stop both versions from repeating secrecy for opposite reasons.
- **POV / want:** Ryu wants a treatment that can be used before the market’s evening crowd is exposed.
- **Pressure:** Younger Tang owns the formula but lacks proof; her brother orders secrecy; future Tang urges theft of her own family’s completed recipe; Oh Gak’s symptoms mutate because he survived.
- **Legal future knowledge:** Future Tang remembers the original Green Vein formulation and her own failed self-test, but this batch contains a standardized binder she never saw at this date.
- **Divergence:** Saving Oh Gak caused the smugglers to alter the poison barrels; neither echo knows the new dosage.
- **Choice:** Ryu refuses future Tang’s demand to steal and gives younger Tang custody of the sample. Younger Tang chooses to publish a bounded emergency protocol, retaining the complete family formula.
- **Local payoff:** They identify a treatment from symptoms and current testing, not prophecy; Oh Gak walks unaided; future Tang admits her younger self’s compassion is expertise, not weakness.
- **Delta:** Tang controls medical handling; emergency protocol exists; dual-seal proposal drafted; Tang core can be channeled for seven heartbeats.
- **Coalition progress:** Medicine is granted explicit authority distinct from Azure Peak security.
- **Persistence:** Tang Jun may punish Soryeong; the poison warehouse must be entered before evening distribution.
- **Next pressure:** Black Tooth begins moving barrels while Azure Peak and Tang argue over who leads the raid.

## Chapter 5 — 둘이 들고 하나가 여는 문

- **Near promise:** Ryu enters the warehouse with two rival teams under divided authority and creates Returning Dish Step while carrying a sloshing poison barrel through a formation corridor.
- **POV / want:** Ryu wants the barrels, buyer records, and workers alive—not merely a destroyed warehouse.
- **Pressure:** Younger Baek wants a fast strike; Tang wants quarantine; workers are coerced; the corridor reacts to single-school internal energy; Ryu’s two cores become unsafe when combined.
- **Legal future knowledge:** Ryu remembers the warehouse layout before renovation; Baek knows sword traps; Tang knows poison handling. None knows the new relay formation or moved barrels.
- **Divergence:** The surviving courier’s warning causes Black Tooth to move the evidence and activate a mixed-energy trap early.
- **Choice:** Ryu divides command—Nam controls exits, Tang controls bodies and barrels, Baek takes the trap line—and risks only three shared heartbeats to redirect the formation instead of breaking it.
- **Local payoff:** Returning Dish Step is born from keeping a barrel level; coerced workers survive; the local boss is defeated; standardized western supply marks are recovered.
- **Delta:** Warehouse evidence enters dual custody; Ryu owns his first original movement; Myogak opens a sealed floor passage but remains unseen.
- **Coalition progress:** Divided authority works under pressure and preserves more than either faction’s original plan.
- **Persistence:** Ryu has torn meridians and cannot overdrive again without rest; Black Tooth’s remaining cell targets the market; the recovered buyer record is incomplete.
- **Next pressure:** Poisoned oil has already reached the night market, where destroying it would ignite the crowd.

## Chapter 6 — 첫 번째 공동 장부

- **Near promise:** The first coalition system must work while Ryu is physically limited; two future masters and two younger versions must trust distributed authority.
- **POV / want:** Ryu wants every market lane warned and treated before the poisoned oil is heated.
- **Pressure:** He cannot cover all lanes or safely channel both cores; Black Tooth’s lieutenant rings false evacuation bells; sect pride threatens conflicting orders; Ma refuses to let Return Inn become an unaccountable command post.
- **Legal future knowledge:** Ryu knows the original death locations because he personally recovered bodies and compiled the casualty ledger afterward, but his changed warning route alters crowd movement. Baek knows command failures; Tang knows symptoms. Exact living positions are new.
- **Divergence:** People Ryu saved become messengers; the enemy adapts to the joint raid and uses false signals.
- **Choice:** Ryu stays at the inn to coordinate instead of chasing the lieutenant. Younger Baek gives Nam route command; younger Tang releases the emergency protocol; Ma controls copies, keys, and costs. Both borrowed channels were spent in the warehouse, so Ryu uses only his own body, Returning Dish Step, and present observation to stop the final ignition without abandoning coordination.
- **Local payoff:** The market survives; Oh Gak captures the lieutenant by using the gang’s own bell code; both future echoes acknowledge a solution neither achieved.
- **Delta:** Return Inn stores the Common Antidote Ledger under Azure Peak and Tang seals; emergency access rules, escort routes, and replenishment duties are signed; Baek and Tang cores stabilize at foundation level.
- **Coalition progress:** First operating institution that functions through divided authority and shared custody.
- **Persistence:** Return Inn is now visible to major factions and the western supply network; Ma charges both factions; Ryu remains physically injured but stronger at baseline.
- **Ending payoff:** In the inner inn, Baek and Tang sit at the same table without agreement. A third locked guest-room door knocks, and one pre-awakening sentence leaks through asking who altered the buyer record. Gwi Sam cannot converse or lend power yet.
- **Next pressure:** Find Gwi Sam’s younger self before the information is sold three different ways.

## Volume continuity summary

- Time elapsed: three days.
- Awakened echoes: Baek in Chapter 1, Tang in Chapter 2; both remain active.
- Living masters met: younger Baek in Chapter 3, younger Tang in Chapter 4.
- Ryu’s original technique: Returning Dish Step in Chapter 5, used under altered conditions in Chapter 6.
- Institutional result: Common Antidote Ledger, not a general alliance.
- Divine Cult visibility: standardized supply marks and doctrine fragment only; no central villain confrontation.
- Myogak: one unseen mechanical response beneath Return Inn.
- Final hook: Gwi Sam’s room begins to wake.
