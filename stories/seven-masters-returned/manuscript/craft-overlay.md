# Craft overlay — Seven Masters Returned

This overlay supplements the repository fiction standard. It does not replace it.

## Primary experience

A fast, rewarding murim regression serial in which an underestimated inn worker begins with a decisive knowledge advantage, gains seven increasingly lively future masters, humiliates early threats through technical superiority, and gradually turns personal victories into durable cooperation among rival factions.

## Core pleasures to protect

- Ryu completing an opponent’s logic before the opponent understands the fight.
- Legendary advice translated into practical inn labor.
- Future masters arguing with one another and being embarrassed by younger selves.
- A younger master making a choice their future echo thought impossible.
- An old enemy becoming useful because Ryu understands the cause rather than only the outcome.
- A hidden opportunity used more intelligently than simple possession.
- Visible upgrades to Return Inn and the coalition.
- Ryu combining incompatible truths into a cleaner solution.

## Anti-promises

- Not an exact-event walkthrough for fifteen years.
- Not seven exposition devices praising Ryu.
- Not a harem structured around collecting younger masters.
- Not a story where one duel substitutes for political and institutional change.
- Not a morally simple orthodox-good/demonic-evil map.
- Not a possession fantasy that erases younger people.
- Not a slow weak-to-competent opening; Ryu should become locally formidable quickly.

## Chapter unit gate

Every chapter must earn the next through:

1. a near-term promise within the opening movement;
2. a concrete want and pressure;
3. at least one choice that closes another option;
4. an immediate payoff—knowledge, reversal, relationship, resource, comedy, or power;
5. a persistent delta in custody, trust, access, injury, status, or institutional capacity;
6. a changed next question.

## Ensemble gate

- A spirit’s line must sound identifiable without a dialogue tag in sustained scenes.
- Masters may agree, but agreement must emerge from distinct reasoning.
- Younger and future versions must share recognizable traits without identical behavior.
- Ryu must reject, combine, or reinterpret advice often enough to remain protagonist.
- Ma Yeongsun must retain enforceable material authority.
- Do not introduce a new master until the reader can state the current master’s specialty, flaw, regret, and relationship with Ryu.

## Overpowered fairness

Ryu’s advantage should produce frequent clean wins, but each win must obey one of these channels:

- witnessed future fact from a named archive;
- technical comprehension legally available through an awakened core;
- preparation using present resources;
- opponent habit supported by current evidence;
- help from an ally with agency;
- Ryu’s own trained or invented movement.

A victory fails the gate if it depends on exact knowledge of an event created only by Ryu’s changed timeline.

## Coalition gate

Every faction arc must change behavior, not merely attitude. Valid proof includes:

- shared custody of a resource;
- interoperable signals;
- accepted command limits;
- medical access;
- verified information exchange;
- budget, shelter, or supply commitments;
- a jointly borne cost;
- a procedure that works while leaders disagree.

## Comedy gate

Comedy should come from status collision, personality, practical consequences, or temporal self-recognition. Avoid repeated generic bickering. After a joke, the power relation or emotional understanding should be clearer.

## Time-skip gate

Do not skip the formation of trust merely to reach the invasion. Skip repetition after the decisive behavior has been dramatized. Re-enter through a concrete changed object, relationship, routine, or threat.

## Ending proof

The climax must show that the coalition works when Ryu cannot personally solve every battlefield. His strongest martial act should embody coordination among differences, and the seven living masters must choose cooperation without being overwritten by their echoes.
