# Visual bible — planning boundary

**Status:** no production art authorized
**Working style inheritance:** `return-ink-v1` may be studied, not copied as current canon

## Visual premise

The replacement should preserve the grounded Korean murim ink-wash identity, practical clothing, readable action, and ordinary inn materiality that worked in the predecessor. It must redesign characters for the fifteen-year dual-version structure before any scene generation.

## Required reference system

Every master eventually needs two clearly related but non-identical reference states:

- younger living self at the opening or first appearance;
- future echo after fifteen years of mastery, war, compromise, and regret.

The pair must share stable identity anchors—bone structure, eyes, habitual posture, handedness, and characteristic gesture—while age, scars, clothing, authority, and emotional bearing show the life between them.

Ryu needs:

- opening twenty-year-old inn-worker reference;
- staged core-awakening costume/power changes that do not turn him into a generic crowned cultivation hero;
- consistent practical movements derived from trays, water, accounting, cleaning, and hospitality.

Ma Yeongsun remains visually sturdy, practical, materially authoritative, and older than the young cast. She must not be redesigned as passive, delicate, or ornamental.

## Inner Return Inn

The mental inn should be immediately distinguishable from the real inn without becoming a glowing game interface.

- night or pre-dawn light;
- seven closed guest-room doors with restrained individual material cues;
- a common table that fills as masters awaken;
- an empty counter associated with Ma;
- memory changes expressed through weather, damaged objects, stains, sound, and architecture;
- no floating status windows, class labels, or modern UI.

## Heavenly Demon Divine Cult

Do not use generic all-black villain armor as the only visual language. The Cult should appear institutionally coherent:

- standardized but role-specific equipment;
- integrated units and signals;
- practical logistics;
- visual evidence of order that can be attractive as well as threatening;
- variation across soldiers, administrators, adherents, and conquered territories.

## Retired assets

All files beneath `stories/seven-regressors-fell/assets/` belong to the retired edition. They may be referenced during design review but may not be copied into this story’s future illustration manifest.

Before new visual production:

1. lock ages, factions, clothing, injuries, and awakening order;
2. create fresh younger/future pair sheets;
3. approve Return Inn real/inner location references;
4. define the Cult’s institutional design;
5. create a new style ID and provenance ledger;
6. visually reject identity drift, fake writing, anatomy errors, timeline mistakes, and premature spoilers.
