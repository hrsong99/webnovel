# Story craft overlay — dopamine-first regression reversal

## Intended experience

A fast sequence of anticipation, exact competence, deserved status reversal, acquisition, visible growth, and larger opportunity. The reader should finish each chapter having received a concrete reward, not merely a promise.

## Audience contract

- Korean martial/progression web-novel readers who enjoy regressors, hidden masters, systems, public recognition, and “munchkin” competence.
- The familiar pleasures are delivered directly, while the protagonist is the non-regressor background figure who remembers everyone else’s failed loops.
- The loop ends before Chapter 1. Prior knowledge is bounded to thirty days and becomes less reliable as consequences diverge.

## Story engine

Rehearsed situation → prediction/preemption → reward or public reversal → star-node unlock → deviation creates an unknown problem → Ryu Dan synthesizes an answer no regressor rehearsed.

## Form

Six-chapter escalating mini-arc:

1. control and survival;
2. loot and bodily awakening;
3. public competence and status;
4. synthesis and leadership;
5. adaptation under uncertainty;
6. original victory and ownership of tomorrow.

## Reward contract

- Micro-payoff roughly every 500–900 Korean characters.
- Medium reversal/acquisition/recognition every 1,500–2,000 characters.
- One undeniable major victory per chapter.
- Same-chapter setup/payoff by default.
- Rotate power, resource, status, relationship, control, comedy, and spectacle.
- Every reward changes an ability, possession, relationship, reputation, or available future.

## Voice

- Close third around Ryu Dan.
- Swift Korean web-novel paragraphs with clean causal flow.
- Ryu Dan is quiet, exact, and rarely explains before acting.
- Ma Yeongsun’s dry humor comes from chores, food, money, and practical consequences.
- Formation messages are sparse, short, and consequential.
- Images center on bells, bowls, doors, repeated footsteps, seven stars, and the first unrehearsed dawn.

## Boundaries

- No endless stupid-young-master queue.
- No random jackpot without prior rule or earned input.
- No future knowledge beyond the repeated thirty days.
- No instant physical mastery solely because Ryu Dan remembers.
- No three consecutive chapters dominated by the same reward type.
- No cliffhanger that merely interrupts ordinary action.
- No crowd reaction without a durable social consequence.
- No humiliation aimed at poverty, disability, or helplessness; punch upward at arrogance and abuse.
- No final victory through one more remembered answer. Ryu Dan must perceive the present and invent.

## Chapter release check

1. What near reward is promised by the first quarter?
2. What can the reader predict before Ryu Dan acts?
3. Which choice or competence earns the outcome?
4. What reward is fully delivered now?
5. What remains visibly changed?
6. Which different reward channel opens next?
