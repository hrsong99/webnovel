# Chapter 5 Reviewer Overview

Chapter 5 turns Ryu Dan’s success in the tomb’s outer halls into durable status and alliances at Return Inn. The Poison Sect, Beggars’ Union, and Azure Peak Sect test him through a counterfeit antidote, Gwi Sam’s creditor ledger, and a public sword challenge; he converts each threat into medicine, information, and an official top-ranked Outer Hall position through observation and precise correction. Ma Yeongsun’s admission fees, premium seating, and doubled soup prices keep the humor practical while showing that she can turn a three-faction standoff into inn revenue.

The outer-gate-key auction matters because it never occurred in any loop. Ryu Dan does not pretend to know the winner or price; he reads a false bidder’s hand, the broker’s hesitation, and the trust he has just earned. By trading targeted corrections to the Poison Sect, Beggars’ Union, and Azure Peak—then securing Nam Sogun’s guarantee—he wins through an honest coalition bid instead of exhausting his silver. Prior-loop knowledge has become material for present judgment rather than a future answer key.

The chapter layers distinct rewards at a brisk cadence: counterfeit medicine is exposed, Gwi Sam’s debts are restored, Azure Peak’s challenger is defeated without Ryu Dan drawing a weapon, and the key plus public backing of three factions are secured. Nam Sogun fastening the official badge, So Yagran engraving Ryu Dan’s name in her medicine case, and the Beggars’ Union marking the inn all create lasting social consequences rather than disposable crowd amazement. Ma Yeongsun immediately monetizes the new reputation with a twenty-tael appointment fee.

The broker’s collapse into black sand awakens Myogak, the first enemy who remembers Ryu Dan as the formation’s recording vessel. Myogak marks and abducts Ma Yeongsun, demanding the return of seven lives’ records. Ryu Dan reopens the closing passage with the newly won key instead of waiting for complete preparation, and Nam Sogun, So Yagran, and U Chil follow his directions without hesitation. The ending cleanly pivots from rehearsed pursuit to an unknown enemy, unknown road, and Ryu Dan’s first deliberate chase into tomorrow.
