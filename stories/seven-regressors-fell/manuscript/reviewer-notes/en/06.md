# Chapter 6 Reviewer Overview

Chapter 6 opens with Myogak replaying the seven regressors’ perfected signature attacks. Ryu Dan breaks the Thirteen Falling Clouds, poison cloud, Plum-Shadow Steps, twin daggers, iron palms, One-Point Heaven Spear, and Glacial White River with minimal movements, but not alone. Nam Sogun shifts the sword-light root and spots the altered floor, So Yagran corrects the changed airflow, U Chil applies his improved cipher principle to escape his chain, and Ma Yeongsun identifies the hollow water channel. The allies are earned contributors who correct memories made unreliable by the present rather than passengers waiting to be rescued.

Ryu Dan dismantles Myogak’s perfected combined formation by deliberately taking a shallow shoulder cut and using the delay to desynchronize its shared joint. Myogak then learns from that choice and recombines recorded beginnings from all seven arts into an unprecedented sequence, but invents no genuinely original motion. Ryu Dan is knocked down because his simultaneous predictions of sword and iron palm open the exact gap Myogak attacks. The defeat immediately reveals the limit of selecting or combining faster answers from the past and supplies the necessary pressure for Ryu Dan’s true original invention.

Ma Yeongsun’s flour and her claim to a share of memories earned through nine thousand meals and washings turn Ryu Dan’s attention from history to the room as it exists now. He reads Nam Sogun’s breath, the incense current, an eighth echo, a tilted flagstone, the water film, Myogak’s missing eye, and the untouched eighth place to create the First Tomorrow Step. A plate shard and an unprecedented bell note disrupt Myogak’s prediction, allowing a simple two-finger strike adjusted to the present wound to win. The climax therefore pays off Ryu Dan’s own martial art rather than one more memorized counter.

Ryu Dan gains authority over the return function and immediately dismantles it while retaining his four star nodes, acquired skill in the Meridian of Ten Thousand Rehearsals, and the First Tomorrow Step. Myogak’s Seven-Star Core yields the seven-layer Final Return Record; the tomb’s outer storehouses and passages survive and bind beneath Return Inn, with the inn as their surface gate. Ma Yeongsun counting broken plates first connects world-changing ownership to the inn’s practical economy. After the first real Day 32 dawns, a visibly wounded woman with eight red rings arrives and says there should have been eight regressors, opening a larger mystery on top of the completed victory.
