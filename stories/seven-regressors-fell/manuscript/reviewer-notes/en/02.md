# Chapter 2 Reviewer Overview

Ryu Dan sends three factions one of the false opportunity maps the seven regressors refined while deceiving one another, making them collide at the mountain shrine. While his would-be tails fight over an elixir that does not exist, he recovers Plum-Shadow Steps from the dry-well cache used from loop 8,106 onward; each sole contains twelve dark-iron plates. His knees buckle and the rope climb nearly exhausts him, turning acquisition immediately into training and a demonstration of his body’s limits.

At the Green Snake Apothecary, obtaining the Myriad-Poison Ward Bead immediately leads to a poisoned merchant. Ryu Dan uses the bead to expose a fake antidote, then saves the victim with the detoxification method learned through Tang Soryeong’s 312 failures and her improvements on attempts 313 and 2,005. The fraudulent apothecary is taken to the magistrate and customers remember “Ryu Dan of Return Inn”; the third star node recognizes the bead and method as its foundation but waits for the remaining poison to be cleared before fully opening.

Using Gwi Sam’s noodle-shop signal, Ryu Dan collects ten taels of silver and two ciphered messages. He legally deposits the two-tael principal, proves the interest note forged, and restores the inn wholly to Ma Yeongsun. When six empty-handed Black Tooth thugs invade, he stops trying to lift the shoes and turns their weight into the force and axis of each step, making the men hit and collide with one another as his second star node opens. Ma Yeongsun bills them for one chair, two tables, three plates, and even an old scratch, converting the inn’s liberation into a financial and comic victory.

Neutralizing the residual poison in the medicinal brew fully opens the third star node, giving Ryu Dan a new sense for unfamiliar flows both inside and outside the body. Even after three opportunities, one rescue, restored ownership, and two opened nodes in a day, he decodes the messages by the method seen in loop 4,400. They conceal dead Baek Mugyeol’s blue-jade Azure Peak Token and a note in Ryu Dan’s own hand ordering him to visit the Azure Peak Sect on a day thirty-one that had never existed, opening the first specific mystery beyond his remembered future.
