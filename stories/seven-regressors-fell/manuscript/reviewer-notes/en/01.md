# Chapter 1 Reviewer Overview

The bell rings on the first-ever dawn of day thirty-one, after the 9,431st return has ended. The chapter immediately establishes that all seven regressors died in the Tomb of Seven Returns, stopping the formation, while Ryu Dan—its non-regressor recording vessel—survived with every loop’s memories. Unfamiliar sunlight laid over familiar domestic sounds gives this “first today” equal measures of wonder and unease.

Ryu Dan recites the Black Tooth thug’s 418-times-repeated threat before he can deliver it, catches the doomed bowl on a tray, and folds the man’s saber wrist. He has no inner power, so the scene proves both the limit and strength of his advantage: perfect knowledge of observed habits and angles rather than borrowed physical mastery. He even identifies the gang’s ledger thief, while Ma Yeongsun’s concern for her bowl and insistence on the water chore finish the status reversal with dry humor.

Ryu Dan captures the roof assassin who always killed Ma Yeongsun using a flour pulley, a loosened woodshed door, and rolling firewood. Memories of prior failures let him block both the needle beneath the assassin’s tongue and the acupoint where qi gathers; a bluff that greater force will tighten the door compensates for his remaining physical weakness. Ma Yeongsun survives that hour for the first time in 9,431 loops, and the bounty plus the inn’s new reputation turn the rescue into relationship, resource, and status rewards.

The assassin’s map leads to a Meridian-Cleansing Pill beneath the ninth stable stone. Using the safe method from loop 7,202 and adapting observed breathing and posture to his present body, Ryu Dan gains his first qi and opens the first star node of the Meridian of Ten Thousand Rehearsals. The formation’s message—“Return 9,431 complete. Recording vessel Ryu Dan: alive”—redefines him from a container for others’ failures into a man who survived the first unknown day, while Ma Yeongsun’s demand for a new kitchen door closes the epic change on practical continuity.
