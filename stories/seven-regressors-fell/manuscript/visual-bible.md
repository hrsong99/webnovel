# Visual bible — 회귀자 일곱이 죽고 점소이만 남았다

## Art direction

- **Style ID:** `return-ink-v1`
- **One-line direction:** Korean wuxia graphic-novel illustration painted as disciplined ink wash and muted mineral pigment on warm mulberry paper, with one restrained cinnabar accent and cinematic negative space.
- **Medium and texture:** visible dry-brush edges, paper grain, selective sharp detail at faces/hands/decisive props; atmospheric washes elsewhere.
- **Line quality:** economical and confident rather than sketchy; action is readable by silhouette.
- **Palette:** charcoal, warm paper, worn indigo, muted celadon, dark timber; cinnabar only for return-formation energy, blood-critical details, or the eighth woman’s wrist rings.
- **Lighting:** practical dawn, lamp, overcast exterior, and tomb light. No generic blue/orange blockbuster lighting.
- **Composition:** 16:9 landscape; one decisive action; mobile-safe center; empty breathing room; avoid poster collages.
- **Emotional tone:** restrained competence, dry humor, pressure becoming control.
- **Must avoid:** anime/chibi styling, glossy game splash art, photoreal celebrity resemblance, faux-Chinese writing, UI overlays, subtitles, signatures, watermarks, modern objects, gratuitous gore, uncontrolled magical particle effects.

## Character identity anchors

These are visual-publication decisions derived from prose, not additions to plot canon.

### Ryu Dan

- **Age/build:** 20; lean working body, quick hands, no heroic bulk.
- **Face:** Korean; narrow dark eyes, straight brows, quiet mouth, youthful oval face with slightly hollow cheeks.
- **Hair:** black, center-to-soft-side part, tied in a low practical knot; two short strands may loosen during combat.
- **Posture:** economical, balanced, shoulders relaxed; never poses for admiration.
- **Base clothing:** faded charcoal-gray work hanbok, off-white inner collar, cream apron at Return Inn, cloth shoes; no ornate armor.
- **Chapter changes:** weighted black-iron shoes from Ch.2; torn shoulder in Ch.3; shredded sleeves and corridor cuts in Ch.4–6; Outer Hall badge only after Ch.5.
- **Props:** round wooden serving tray; broken wooden wedge; blue-jade Azure Peak Token when canonically present.
- **Handedness:** actions may use either hand only as the prose specifies; do not arbitrarily swap a held key, weapon, or injured side.
- **Never show:** crown, noble robe, long loose fantasy hair, glowing eyes, oversized sword, muscular cultivation-hero body.
- **Approved reference:** `assets/scenes/references/ryu-dan-v1.webp`

### Ma Yeongsun

- **Age/build:** 43; sturdy working build, strong forearms.
- **Face:** Korean; angular practical face, alert eyes, visible smile lines, expression that prices damage before praising heroism.
- **Hair:** black with a few gray strands, tight no-nonsense bun, one plain silver pin.
- **Clothing:** worn indigo work hanbok and dark apron; flour, water, or dishcloth details when present.
- **Posture:** planted and unflustered even amid martial spectacle.
- **Props:** ledger, dishcloth, flour sack, plate shard depending on scene.
- **Never show:** delicate maiden styling, ornate jewelry, passive fear pose, youthful face.
- **Approved reference:** `assets/scenes/references/ma-yeongsun-v1.webp`

### Nam Sogun

- **Age/build:** early thirties; trained but not massive.
- **Face/hair:** Korean; sun-browned face, direct gaze, black hair in a high martial knot.
- **Clothing:** dark Azure Peak blue martial uniform; thirteen small cloud motifs along one sleeve; practical sword.
- **Posture:** precise examiner’s discipline; skepticism becomes respect, never buffoonish shock.
- **Never show:** as one of the seven regressors, elderly, villain-coded, or incompetent.
- **Approved reference:** `assets/scenes/references/nam-sogun-v1.webp`

### Myogak

- **Nature:** formation-born keeper, not a human monk and not one of the seven regressors.
- **Shape:** spare monk-like silhouette made from matte black sand, ash, and red formation lines; calm mask-like face.
- **Eyes:** seven small black eye-points arranged deliberately across the face before damage; exactly six remain after one falls in Ch.6.
- **Movement:** unsettlingly exact, as if recorded actions have been spliced together; no improvisational flourish.
- **Never show:** ordinary flesh monk, benevolent Buddha iconography, eight eyes, horns, sci-fi robot parts, generic smoke monster.
- **Approved reference:** `assets/scenes/references/myogak-v1.webp`

## Locations

### Return Inn

- Worn roadside Korean timber inn between Qingliu County and Azure Peak Mountain.
- Low beams, paper doors, wood tables, earthen kitchen surfaces, brass bell, stable yard, practical clutter.
- Warm paper/wood palette; damage persists after fights; never a polished palace tavern.

### Azure Peak Sect

- Mountain gate and training court in gray stone and dark timber; cloud motif used sparingly.
- Disciplined blue uniforms; no giant floating palace or Chinese text signage.

### Tomb of Seven Returns

- Ancient gray-black stone, seven-coffin geometry, embedded formation grooves, red lines used only where active.
- Outer vault and passages are material architecture, not a neon game dungeon.
- Blade corridor threat should be shown through pressure, cuts, displaced dust, and narrow highlights rather than hundreds of floating swords.
