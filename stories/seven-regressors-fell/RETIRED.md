## Status

No files were deleted. The Korean source, English edition, reviewer notes, visual bible, approved illustrations, and release evidence remain available as historical artifacts and possible adaptation material.

## Retirement reason

The premise established that Day 31 had never existed, then used exact Day 31 events—the debt collector’s sequence, falling bowl, assassin route, and repeated rescue attempts—as knowledge acquired in earlier loops. The retained-memory rule explained cumulative learning inside the repeated period but could not make an unprecedented day rehearsable. Because the contradiction powered Chapter 1’s main reversals, repairing it required a premise replacement rather than a local wording fix.

## Reuse policy

Nothing in this folder is automatically canon for the replacement. The following may be considered for adaptation after reconciling them with the new one-return, fifteen-year mechanics:

- Ryu Dan and Ma Yeongsun;
- Return Inn and practical domestic comedy;
- the seven named martial traditions;
- tray, bowl, weighted-shoe, poison, vault, auction, and formation set pieces;
- mundane labor becoming original martial movement;
- `return-ink-v1` visual direction after redesigned references.

The following must not transfer:

- the 9,431 short loops;
- first-ever Day 31 chronology;
- claims that Ryu rehearsed events outside the loop window;
- recording-vessel omniscience;
- predecessor continuity ledgers, placements, or character references treated as current canon.

For the accepted replacement premise and future authoring state, begin at `stories/seven-masters-returned/README.md`.
